Understanding the Plots

  • Calibration: Compares your predicted confidence to actual success rate. Perfect calibration follows the diagonal. Points above = overconfident, below = underconfident.
  • Brier Score: Measures calibration over time. Lower is better (0 = perfect, 0.25 = random guessing).
  • Confidence: Distribution of your confidence levels, colored by outcome. Helps spot patterns.
  • Reasons: Success rate by reason type. Shows which reasoning approaches work best for you.
